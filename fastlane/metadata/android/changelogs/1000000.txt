First release.

* Connects to a self-hosted Readeck or Wallabag server, using your own API
  credentials. There is no NeverReader account and nothing is sent anywhere
  except your own server.
* Unread, favorites and archive lists, with search, sorting and tags.
* Highlights and annotations on saved articles.
* Reads offline from the on-device cache, and syncs in the background through
  WorkManager, pushing your changes upstream and pulling new ones down.
* Light and dark themes, adjustable reader text size, and in-app article reader.
* No analytics, crash reporting, ads or telemetry of any kind.
* Forked from the archived Pocket Android app, with the Pocket backend and all
  tracking removed.
