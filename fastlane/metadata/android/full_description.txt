NeverReader is a read-it-later client for a server you run yourself. It connects to
Readeck or Wallabag over their own APIs and reads your list from there, so your
saved articles stay on your own hardware.

There is no NeverReader account, and no analytics, crash reporting or telemetry of
any kind: the app requests only network access, plus the background permissions
Android's own WorkManager needs to sync on a schedule.

Features:

* Unread, favorites and archive lists, with search and sorting
* Highlights and annotations on saved articles
* Offline reading, with the list cached on the device
* Background sync that pushes your changes upstream and pulls new ones down
* Light and dark themes, and adjustable text size for the reader
* Open articles in your browser or read them in the built-in reader

NeverReader is a fork of the archived Pocket Android app, with the Pocket backend
and all tracking removed.
