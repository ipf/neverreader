# Credits

NeverReader is a fork of [Pocket/pocket-android](https://github.com/Pocket/pocket-android).
Pocket was shut down in July 2025, and the work that came out of it — removing
the sync engine, the analytics and the View-based UI, and building the Readeck
and Wallabag backends — is not attributable to anyone who worked on the app as
published.

## The Pocket app

Because the code was imported with its history, the
[contributors page](https://github.com/Pocket/pocket-android/graphs/contributors)
on the upstream repository is the accurate record of who wrote the code that
this fork started from. It is also why this file is short: listing the
NeverReader-specific contributors separately would be a rolling list, and the
upstream page is the one that matters for provenance.

## Bundled fonts

The brand faces in the original app, Graphik LCG and Doyle, were commercially
licensed and are not distributable. They are replaced by
[Inter](https://rsms.me/inter/) and [Source Serif 4](https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Source+Serif+4),
both OFL-1.1. Their licenses are in
[`ui/src/main/assets/fonts`](ui/src/main/assets/fonts).
